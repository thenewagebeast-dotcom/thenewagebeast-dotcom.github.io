What the codes mean

Every recipe carries a few short codes as tags. Here's what each one means, and a couple of other things worth knowing.

Recipe tags
  • HP - High Protein
  • MP - Meal Prep Friendly
  • BC - Batch Cook Friendly
  • FF - Freezer Friendly
  • MF - Microwave Friendly
  • LB - Lunchbox Friendly
  • Q - Quick, around 30 minutes or less
  • BF - Budget Friendly
  • AF - Air Fryer Friendly
  • GF - Gluten-Free Adaptable (easy swaps, check the ingredients for the swap)
  • V - Vegetarian (no meat or fish; eggs and dairy-free yoghurt are fine)
  • DF - Dairy-Free (genuinely zero dairy, a step stronger than just lactose-free)
  • KS - Keep Separate (parts of this dish should be stored or packed separately, not mixed ahead of time)
  • KT - Keep Toppings Separate (add the crunchy or fresh topping just before eating, not in advance)
Lactose-free vs dairy-free
Every recipe in this book is lactose-free. DF is a stronger claim than that, it only appears when a recipe is genuinely dairy-free by default, with no dairy option built in at all. If a recipe offers "coconut yoghurt or lactose-free yoghurt" as a choice, for example, it's still fully lactose-free, it just won't carry the DF tag, since the lactose-free version does still contain dairy.
Other things you'll see
  • A small dot in the corner of a recipe card means you've left a cooking note on it. Tap "Manage notes & photos" on the main screen to see, export, or import both.
  • The Spice level chips let you filter by heat, separately from the tag chips above.
  • Tapping "add photo" or "change photo" on a recipe saves the picture to this device, but like notes, it can be exported and shared from "Manage notes & photos".
  • "Plan this week's shopping" lets you tick off what you're cooking, set servings, and it builds one combined shopping list. Your picks are saved automatically, so reopening it shows what you last planned.
  • Inside a recipe, "Keep screen on while cooking" stops your phone from locking while you're following the method. It turns off automatically when you leave the recipe.
